Get Nationally Certified at BELART Assessment Center

Validate your skills with official TESDA National Certification (NC) examinations. Our certified assessors ensure fair, rigorous, and industry-aligned evaluations.

How to Take NC Assessment

  • 1 Choose your qualification and select an assessment date.
  • 2 Submit credentials (TOR, work certificate, or training completion).
  • 3 Settle assessment fees online or onsite upon confirmation.
  • 4 Complete the theoretical exam and hands-on practical demonstration.
